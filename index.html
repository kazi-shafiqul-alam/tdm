Our Mission

From planetary data to better exploration decisions.

Tour de Mars brings mapping context, terrain awareness, scientific targets, route choices and a visible return-to-base constraint into one coherent exploration story. MarsWalk is a research prototype, not an operational navigation system.

Challenge Context

Built as an independent Space Apps project.

Tour de Mars is an independent student/team project for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. The site uses NASA and planetary-science resources for exploration and prototyping; it is not an official NASA product or a certified human-mission safety tool.
